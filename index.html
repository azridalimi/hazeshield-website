Featured Project: HazeShield

Exploring smart agriculture solutions in response to haze and air-quality challenges.

HazeShield Indoor Farm

HazeShield is a green technology and smart agriculture project concept focused on protecting plant-growing environments from challenges associated with haze. The project explores environmental monitoring and controlled growing conditions to support plant growth.

Areas being explored include:

  • Air-quality and environmental monitoring.
  • IoT sensors and environmental data collection.
  • Controlled indoor growing environments.
  • Technology-assisted observation of plant conditions.

HazeShield is an innovation project under development. Its performance and effectiveness should be established through testing and evaluation.
